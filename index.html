What we do

Help for the people & businesses around us

JKLM Collective is a community-focused collective based in Manitoba. It helps job seekers, small businesses & families with the digital & practical work that is hard to do alone.

I. Compass

Career services

Resumes, interview practice & job-search plans for people starting out or changing paths.

II. Bridges

Small business support

Brand guides, posters & day-to-day systems for local businesses getting off the ground.

III. Navigation

Digital & tech services

Websites, online listings & tech setup so people & businesses can be found online.

IV. Tribute

Memorial services

Memorial programs, displays & slideshows, made with care for families honouring a life.
